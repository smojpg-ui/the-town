this is the town off the edge — the one we built one mark at a time. it isn't finished. it isn't meant to be.
  • hover a place to wake it.
  • click it to keep it open here.
  • every place has roads. walk one, and the two of you go along.
  • the layers under the map are the town's threads. switch one on and a red string runs through its places; walk it for the tour.
  • the compass in the top corner is ours. it only looks like a real one.
  • the blank edge stays blank on purpose. that part's yours.
